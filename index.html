Everything your company knows, connected to everything it does.

The Problem

Companies mistake activity for outcomes.

Knowledge is scattered across documents and systems, while execution depends on people remembering procedures. Someone clicks “done,” a workflow advances, and management assumes the result is complete. Companies don't need another knowledge base — they need an execution engine that owns the outcome and proves it happened.

The Category

The operational control plane for the organization.

Not a replacement for every tool, and not a passive search layer over your files — a connected operating model that owns execution and earns operational authority.

What it is

  • The operational control plane for the organization.
  • A semantic, temporal model connecting people, documents, money, policies and work.
  • The runtime for Company Skills and governed AI agents.
  • The management system for reviews, exceptions, decisions and follow-through.
  • A native workspace for official records, procedures and connected files.

What it is not

  • Not a generic chatbot that answers questions without owning outcomes.

  • Not only a search layer over existing files.

  • Not unrestricted software allowed to move money on model confidence alone.

In Action

A morning with AI COO.

Less like automation, more like your most competent operator: it knows what should happen, prepares the work, asks the right person at the right time, and escalates only what matters.

AI COO — Daily Brief

Decisions requested

  • Approve or pause Data Works — April hours are 28% above plan; the cap runs out in 11 days and no contract amendment exists.
  • Renew the insurance policy before Friday's notice deadline — premium up 12%, cyber limit below the new customer requirement.
  • Approve the revised hiring sequence — moving two roles to October keeps the cash low point above the $600K policy threshold.

New risks

  • A supplier tax certificate expired — two payments totaling $18,700 are blocked; an automatic renewal request was sent.
  • A customer milestone is five days late, delaying a $42,000 invoice unless acceptance lands by Thursday.
  • A database backup-restore test failed — IT has a remediation task due today.

Handled automatically

  • Prepared nine supplier payments; routed two for approval.
  • Created accounts and equipment requests for two new employees.
  • Reminded three contracts entering the 60-day renewal window.
  • Updated the cash forecast with approved invoices and hourly-supplier accruals.

What can wait

  • Four minor document-classification questions remain in the queue — none affect an active payment, contract deadline or compliance obligation this week.

The Initial Wedge

Finance & operations control, starting with the supplier payment.

Where documents, money, approvals, recurring reviews and accountability intersect. Before a single payment moves, AI COO validates the full picture — performing every possible check itself and routing the rest by company procedure.

  • Active supplier agreement
  • Contract validity
  • Tax registration
  • Withholding tax certificate
  • Whether withholding tax applies
  • Payment policy
  • Correct bank account & entity
  • Budget availability
  • Required approvals

The result

Every check is verified with evidence, the remaining tasks are routed with full context and a clear deadline, and nothing is paid until every constraint is satisfied. Each run enriches supplier history, contract usage, budget and the cash forecast.
