The Problem

Companies mistake activity for outcomes.

Knowledge is scattered across documents and systems, while execution depends on people remembering procedures. Someone clicks “done,” a workflow advances, and management assumes the result is complete. Companies don't need another knowledge base — they need an execution engine that owns the outcome and proves it happened.

The Category

The operational control plane for the organization.

Not a replacement for every tool, and not a passive search layer over your files — a connected operating model that owns execution and earns operational authority.

What it is

  • The operational control plane for the organization.
  • A semantic, temporal model connecting people, documents, money, policies and work.
  • The runtime for Company Skills and governed AI agents.
  • The management system for reviews, exceptions, decisions and follow-through.
  • A native workspace for official records, procedures and connected files.

What it is not

  • Not a generic chatbot that answers questions without owning outcomes.
  • Not only a workflow builder where every branch is designed by hand.
  • Not only a search layer over existing files.
  • Not a replacement for every ledger, payroll engine or CRM on day one.
  • Not unrestricted software allowed to move money on model confidence alone.

The AI Executive Layer

One company model. A team of AI executives.

AI COO is the primary generalist executive — it coordinates cross-functional operations and maintains follow-through. Specialist executives add domain depth without becoming independent agents with separate memories or permissions. They all operate through the same operating system, policy framework and audit trail.

How it works

From event to verified outcome.

The same engine that reviews a payment can run an employee offboarding or a security incident. Breadth comes from shared infrastructure and reusable Skills — not unrelated features.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Detect the business event the moment it occurs.

  2. 02

    Apply Skills

    Launch the correct versioned Company Skill with full context.

  3. 03

    Check

    Validate every constraint before anything executes.

  4. 04

    Coordinate

    Run automations and delegate tasks to the right people.

  5. 05

    Verify

    Require evidence that the intended result actually happened.

  6. 06

    Report

    Surface decisions, risks and follow-through to management.

Constraint Engine

Decision Engine

Evidence-Based Completion

Distinguishes activity from proof. Every material step defines what evidence closes it.

Bounded Autonomy

A credible path from assisted execution to trusted autonomy.

Autonomy is measured by safe, verified outcomes — never by the percentage of steps performed by AI. The executives prepare complete evidence, prevent a bad payment, and ask the one decisive question. Nothing moves money or changes access on model confidence alone.

Hard constraints

Execution is blocked. No exceptions.

Soft constraints

Approval is required before proceeding.

Recommendations

Surfaced as guidance — never forced.

Roadmap

Gated by capability and trust — not the calendar.

  1. Phase 0

    Design partner

  2. Phase 1

  3. Phase 2

    Supplier onboarding, contract renewal, budget and cash forecasting, payment execution support and monthly close.

  4. Phase 3

    Employee lifecycle, customer billing and collections, projects, incidents, assets, access and policy management.

  5. Phase 4

    Autonomous enterprise platform

    Specialist AI executives, simulation, a Skill marketplace, jurisdiction packs, and higher bounded autonomy earned through reliability.
