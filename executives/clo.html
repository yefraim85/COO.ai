In Action

When legal usually fails operationally — and doesn't here.

  1. 01

    Obligation

    Each renewal date, payment term and liability cap is extracted from the signed text — with a link back and a confidence score.

  2. 02

    Constraint

    Approved obligations generate events and constraints; an expired certificate blocks the related payment.

  3. 03

    Notice

    Renewal and notice periods fire as timed events, so deadlines are met — not missed.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Approvals, acknowledgments and remediation are captured as evidence for the next audit.

The guardrail

Traceable interpretation — never a false guarantee.

Contract intelligence stays traceable: each extracted fact links back to the signed text and carries confidence, and material interpretations can require legal validation. Installing a compliance pack or template never automatically guarantees legal compliance — every pack carries its source authority, version, effective date and maintenance responsibility.
