The guardrail

A particularly strong trust model.

Security actions demand the strictest guardrails: least privilege, allowlisted tools, short-lived credentials, read/write separation, and independent approval for high-risk changes. AI CISO may analyze and coordinate, but the central policy engine controls access and action — never model confidence alone.
