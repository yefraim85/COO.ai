The guardrail

Cash forecasting that proposes actions, not just numbers.

When a threshold is crossed, AI CFO identifies the assumptions and obligations causing the low point, proposes collection, spend, timing or financing actions, and shows their effect — with ranges and confidence. Payment execution is phased and safe: the platform prepares a complete payment pack; direct initiation requires strong identity, dual control, transaction limits and reconciliation.
