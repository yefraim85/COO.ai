People & organization

AI CHRO — the employee lifecycle, with every dependency handled.

People operations are naturally cross-functional. Hiring affects budget, contracts, payroll, equipment, identity, training and org design. Offboarding affects access, assets, knowledge, customers and final settlement. AI CHRO connects these through shared entities and events — not an isolated HR record system.

The guardrail

Employment facts and human judgment, kept distinct.

The Digital Twin distinguishes employment facts from inferred performance. A role, manager, contract and access profile are structured facts; productivity or engagement is uncertain and sensitive. AI CHRO can summarize operational signals, but consequential people decisions require human judgment, fair process and appropriate legal review.
