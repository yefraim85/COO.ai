The Problem

Knowledge is everywhere. Execution depends on people remembering.

Procedures live scattered across documents, inboxes, and systems — while getting things done relies on the right person recalling the right steps at the right moment. Companies don't need another place to store knowledge. They need an engine that runs it.

The Solution

From knowing what to do — to it being done.

AI COO detects business events, launches the correct workflows, executes automations, delegates tasks, verifies completion, and escalates when needed.

  1. 01

    Detect

    Business events are recognized the moment they occur.

  2. 02

    Launch

    The correct workflow starts automatically, with the right context.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Automations run and tasks are delegated to the right people.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Completion is confirmed — and anything stuck is escalated.

The Platform

A living model of your company — with a mind for how it runs.

Constraint Engine

Every operational event is validated before execution — so standards and compliance are enforced by default, not by memory.

Three levels of control

Hard Constraints

Execution is blocked. No exceptions.

Soft Constraints

Approval is required before proceeding.

Recommendations

Suggestions only — surfaced, never forced.

In Action

Example: a supplier payment request arrives.

Before a single shekel moves, AI COO validates the full picture — automatically. It performs every possible action itself and assigns the rest according to company procedure.

  • Active supplier agreement
  • Contract validity
  • Tax registration
  • Withholding tax certificate
  • Whether withholding tax applies
  • Payment policy
  • Correct bank account & entity
  • Budget availability
  • Required approvals

The result

AI COO performs all possible checks and actions automatically, then routes the remaining tasks to the right people — with full context and a clear deadline. Nothing is paid until every constraint is satisfied.
